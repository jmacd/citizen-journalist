## Division of Drinking Water

**To:** `DDW-PRARequest@waterboards.ca.gov`
**Subject:** Complete permit file for CA2300584 and November 26, 2025 permit amendment

Please provide the complete Division of Drinking Water permit and project file
for public water system **CA2300584, Mendocino School District–Mendocino**,
concerning the Water System Reconstruction Project and the water-supply permit
amendment approved November 26, 2025, including:

1. The complete November 26, 2025 amended domestic water supply permit,
   transmittal letter, findings, exhibits, attachments, conditions, and all
   documents incorporated by reference.
2. The permit immediately preceding that amendment and every prior amendment
   remaining operative, incorporated, or superseded by the November 2025 permit.
3. The completed permit-amendment application and every supplement or revision.
4. The January 15, 2020 GHD Water System Plan Report and all later engineering
   reports, design reports, hydraulic calculations, fire-flow and storage
   calculations, plans, specifications, review comments, approvals, and
   approved or stamped plan sets.
5. All source-approval records for Wells 1, 2, 6, and 7 and any additional
   proposed or test wells, including source assessments, completion reports,
   sanitary surveys, siting approvals, pump tests, water-quality analyses,
   sanitary-seal approvals, GWUDI determinations, and authorization to connect
   or operate.
6. The January 25, 2021 GHD Well 6 technical memorandum, April 19, 2023
   hydrogeologic report, October 7, 2024 revised hydrogeologic report, and every
   DDW technical review.
7. Every emergency or hauled-water operating plan addressing activation
   triggers, eligible customers, delivery area, allocations, available volumes,
   minimum reserves, truck filling, hydrants, metering, backflow or air-gap
   protection, disinfection, sampling, licensed haulers, fill logs, public or
   private receiving tanks, and fire-department access.
8. Every service-area or customer description reviewed for the permit,
   including maps or discussion of the MCCSD boundary, County Emergency Water
   Service Area, or Slaughterhouse Gulch watershed boundary.
9. Correspondence among DDW, MUSD, MCCSD, GHD, Mendocino County, DWR, and DFA
   concerning the permit amendment.
10. A permit history identifying issue dates and supersession relationships for
    every permit and amendment for CA2300584.

The signed Notice of Determination posted as SCH 2020080439 document 12 states
that records of permit approval are available to the general public upon
request at the DDW office. Please provide responsive records in their existing
native electronic formats, including attachments and embedded GIS or plan
files. Rolling production is acceptable.
